## 2026-05-29 - Enhanced Focus Visibility on Video Streams
**Learning:** Interactive elements overlaid on dark backgrounds or video streams (like in CameraView) require high-contrast focus states using large, contrasting offsets (e.g., focus-visible:ring-offset-black) for clear visibility.
**Action:** Always combine focus-visible:ring with contrasting ring-offsets when placing buttons over unpredictable backgrounds.
