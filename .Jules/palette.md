## 2026-05-22 - AR Interaction Accessibility and Dark Background Focus Styles
**Learning:** AR targeting views (like CameraView) present unique accessibility challenges because the targeting state is primarily conveyed visually through dynamic on-screen markers, making it inaccessible to screen reader users. Additionally, when overlaying controls on live camera feeds or dark backgrounds, default focus styles often have insufficient contrast and get lost in the noise.
**Action:** Always provide dynamic `aria-label` properties on AR capture controls to explicitly announce the targeting state (e.g., 'No target locked', 'Capture [Target Name]', 'Already discovered') to provide essential context for visually impaired users. Furthermore, ensure high-contrast focus states for all overlaid buttons by combining `focus-visible:ring-*` with large, contrasting offsets (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-offset-black` or `focus-visible:ring-offset-brand-bg`) to guarantee visibility against complex or dark backgrounds.
