## 2024-06-02 - Dynamic ARIA Labels and High-Contrast Focus Rings for AR Camera Overlays
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons with dynamically changing states (e.g., an AR capture button changing from 'Capture' to 'Saving' to 'Already Collected') require dynamic `aria-label` attributes to maintain context for screen readers. Furthermore, interactive elements overlaid on dark backgrounds or video streams (like in `CameraView`) require high-contrast focus rings with contrasting ring offsets (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-offset-black`) to ensure clear visibility during keyboard navigation.
**Action:** When building interactive overlays for camera or media streams, always implement dynamic `aria-label` attributes that accurately reflect the current state of multi-purpose buttons, and consistently apply `focus-visible:ring-offset-black` to ensure focus indicators remain visible against varied or dark backgrounds.
