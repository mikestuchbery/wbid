## 2024-06-16 - Contextual ARIA labels and Dark Overlay Focus Offsets
**Learning:** Video AR components require dynamic ARIA labels based on target locking state, and their interactive elements over dark streams need high-contrast ring offsets (`focus-visible:ring-offset-black`) to ensure keyboard focus visibility.
**Action:** Always implement contextual ARIA states for multi-purpose capture buttons and use high-contrast ring offsets for overlays on media streams.
