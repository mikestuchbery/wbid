## 2024-05-30 - High-Contrast Focus States for Dynamic AR/Media Overlays
**Learning:** Standard keyboard focus states (like a simple outer ring) frequently fail color contrast requirements when applied to buttons overlaid on dynamic, dark, or video backgrounds (e.g., AR camera feeds or dark glassmorphism cards). Additionally, dynamically changing icon buttons (like a capture button whose context depends on the AR target) require dynamic `aria-label`s to remain accessible.
**Action:** When implementing interactive elements over media streams or dark UI components, always enforce high-contrast focus rings by pairing the focus ring with a contrasting ring offset (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-brand-accent focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-black`). Also, ensure ARIA labels on dynamic action buttons reflect their current target/state.
