## 2026-05-27 - High-Contrast Focus Rings for Dark Backgrounds
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons on complex or dark backgrounds (like camera feeds or dark themes) require high-contrast focus states for keyboard accessibility, as standard focus rings can blend in.
**Action:** Use `focus-visible:ring-offset-black` or `focus-visible:ring-offset-brand-bg` combined with appropriate ring colors to ensure focus rings are clearly visible on dark/glass surfaces.
